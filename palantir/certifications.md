## Proctored certification exams
- Palantir publishes exam guides (Data Engineer, Application Developer, Solution Architect) on Palantir Learn; exams run through Palantir's certification site.
- Registration has typically required an **access or promo code**, often provided through employers and partners; individuals on the community forum report difficulty getting one. *(check the current process)*
- Ways to try:
  1. Ask on the Palantir Community how independent learners can register.
  2. ALTEN recruits Skywise / Foundry developers: ask, through the right internal contact, whether a partner exam code is possible for an employee.
  3. Once at Airbus, ask the Skywise team about certification paths.
- Prepare with the exam guide anyway: it is the best syllabus available.
