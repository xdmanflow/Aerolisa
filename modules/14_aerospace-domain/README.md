# Module 14 · Aerospace & ASD domain

**Roles:** all · **Size:** parallel track, mostly on the commute (~20 min/day) + 1 Saturday to consolidate · **Priority:** P0 · **Builds:** Aerolisa M11 (case study), every interview

## Why this matters
Skywise apprentices work with maintenance engineers, reliability teams and airline customers. A data person who speaks their language (ATA chapters, MEL, AOG, removals) gets trusted faster and interviews better. It's also what turns Aerolisa from "a Kaggle project" into "a fleet decision tool".

## 🎯 Apprenticeship level (must)
**Airbus and the market**
- [ ] Airbus divisions: Commercial Aircraft, Defence and Space, Helicopters; Toulouse sites; subsidiaries and joint ventures relevant to data (e.g. Airbus Atlantic, ATR)
- [ ] Commercial families: A220, A320 family (A319/A320/A321, neo), A330 (neo), A350; what a backlog and a production rate are
- [ ] Skywise: what it is (aviation data platform built with Palantir), Core, and the fleet-performance products (S.Health Monitoring, S.Predictive Maintenance+, S.Fleet Reliability, S.Fleet Performance+); who uses it (airlines, MROs, Airbus internally). See [career/airbus.md](../../career/airbus.md).

**Airline operations**
- [ ] Flight, sector, cycle, block time vs flight time, utilization; turnaround; on-time performance and delay codes
- [ ] Maintenance Control Centre (MCC), dispatch, **MEL** (minimum equipment list), deferred defects, **AOG**

**Maintenance & reliability**
- [ ] Line vs base maintenance; A-check and C-check; maintenance programme (MPD/AMP), task cards
- [ ] **ATA chapters** (iSpec 2200): know the main ones (21 air conditioning, 32 landing gear, 36 pneumatic, 49 APU, 71–80 powerplant…)
- [ ] Scheduled vs unscheduled removals, MTBUR / MTBF, no-fault-found, reliability programmes, alert levels
- [ ] Engines: modules, shop visits, on-wing time, engine condition / health monitoring, EGT margin
- [ ] Aircraft data: ACARS, ACMS, fault messages, FOMAX; why the data is messy (gaps, units, codes)
- [ ] MRO software and ERP: AMOS, TRAX, SAP (work orders, materials, notifications) at concept level

**Regulation and constraints**
- [ ] EASA: Part-21 (design/production), Part-M / Part-CAMO (continuing airworthiness), Part-145 (maintenance organisations), Part-147 (training); FAA as the US counterpart
- [ ] Airworthiness directives, service bulletins
- [ ] Defence & space constraints: security clearance eligibility (habilitation in France), export control (ITAR / EAR in the US); why some data never leaves its network

**Space**
- [ ] Satellites (Earth observation, telecom, navigation), launchers (Ariane 6), ground segment; data volumes in Earth observation

## 🚀 Pro level
- [ ] Sustainability data: fuel burn, SAF, CO₂ per passenger-km, contrails
- [ ] Supply chain and production data (Airbus ramp-up, supplier performance)
- [ ] Flight test data (ATR / Airbus flight-test data processing)

## Steps
1. **Commute (every day):** one topic from the list per day; one Airbus newsroom article; add 3 terms to the `Aviation` Anki deck.
2. **Glossary:** keep [glossary.md](glossary.md) current: term, one-line definition in my words, where it matters for data.
3. **Saturday consolidation (once):** write `work/primer.md`: a 2-page explanation of "why predictive maintenance saves money" using correct vocabulary, as if to a new data colleague.
4. **Apply:** Aerolisa `docs/case-study.md` and the vocabulary in every README.

## Exit test (45 min, no AI)
40-term glossary quiz; explain a predictive maintenance business case in 2 minutes; explain what S.Health Monitoring, S.Predictive Maintenance+ and S.Fleet Reliability each do.
