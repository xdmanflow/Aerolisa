# STAR stories

Five stories cover nearly every behavioural question. Each: **S**ituation (2 lines) · **T**ask (1 line) · **A**ction (3–5 lines, what *I* did) · **R**esult (a number or a concrete outcome) · **Lesson** (1 line). Write them myself, then rehearse in English and in French.

| # | Theme it covers | Raw material from my experience |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learning fast / challenge | Studying engineering fully in English after moving from Algiers to Toulouse, and finishing top of my class |
| 2 | Failure / adaptation | An ALTEN project that was cancelled (CV extraction automation): what I did with the work and how I pivoted |
| 3 | Delivering for non-technical stakeholders | ALTEN dashboards and analyses for recruitment stakeholders (France and UK) |
| 4 | Data quality / rigour | Cleaning messy interview or candidate data at ALTEN; an error I caught |
| 5 | Initiative / teamwork | Building Aerolisa on my own time; or the ALTEN Solidaire collection |
| 6 (spare) | Research / persistence | SAA predictive maintenance research on C-MAPSS |

Only use ALTEN details my tutor is comfortable with; never real figures that are confidential.
