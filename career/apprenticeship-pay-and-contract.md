# Apprenticeship pay & contract (France)

> Not legal advice: a summary of public rules as of late 2026, so I know which questions to ask. Confirm every figure with the employer's HR, CESI and service-public.fr before signing.

## How the minimum is set
- The legal minimum is a **percentage of the SMIC**, depending on **age** and **year of the contract** (Code du travail, art. D6222-26).
- Age bands: under 18 · 18–20 · **21–25** · 26 and over. The 21–25 band is mine for the whole contract.
- For 21–25: **53%** of the SMIC in the 1st year of the contract, **61%** in the 2nd, **78%** in the 3rd.
- From 21, the percentage applies to the **higher** of the SMIC and the **salaire minimum conventionnel** of the job in the employer's collective agreement.
- The SMIC changed during 2026; use the current monthly figure when computing.
- Employers can pay more than the minimum. Large companies often do, through their own agreements.

## Airbus specifically *(check)*
- Airbus falls under the metallurgy collective agreement; company agreements and benefits can add to the legal minimum.
- Postings link to Airbus's "reward and benefits" page: read it before the interview.
- The most reliable numbers come from current Airbus apprentices: ask on LinkedIn, politely, after building some rapport.

## Net pay and tax
- Since March 2025, apprentices pay social contributions on the part of their pay above 50% of the SMIC.
- Apprentice pay is exempt from income tax up to the annual SMIC amount.

## Also ask about
- [ ] Public transport reimbursement (employer share of the pass)
- [ ] Profit-sharing schemes (intéressement / participation) and whether apprentices are eligible
- [ ] Company restaurant, works-council (CSE) benefits
- [ ] Remote-work rules for apprentices
- [ ] Who pays the school costs (normally covered through the employer / OPCO for apprenticeships)

## Before signing
- [ ] Contract type: contrat d'apprentissage; start and end dates match the CESI calendar
- [ ] Tutor (maître d'apprentissage) named
- [ ] Rhythm school / company written and matching CESI's
- [ ] Pay computed by me from the rules above and matching the contract
- [ ] Any paperwork tied to my residence status done before the start date

## After the apprenticeship (for later)
Junior data salaries vary a lot by company, city and role. Sources to compare when the time comes: APEC salary studies, company postings that publish ranges (US postings often do), and conversations with people in the role. Keep the "priority to salary" goal in mind: a strong first brand (Airbus, Skywise, Foundry) is the lever for the best-paid moves later.
