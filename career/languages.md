- **Exam:** TOEIC Listening & Reading (scored out of 990).
- **Why it matters twice:** French engineering degrees (CTI) require a certified B2 level in English; check CESI's exact threshold for my programme. Airbus postings ask English from B2 to "negotiation / fluent".
- **My target:** ≥ 900. My programme is taught in English, so the gap is exam technique and speed, not the language.

**Prep (replaces three Saturday sessions before the exam):**
1. Full official practice test, timed. Score each part. Write the 3 weakest question types.
2. Targeted session: grammar traps (Part 5–6), reading speed and skimming (Part 7), short conversations and talks (Part 3–4) with official material.
3. Second full timed test; review every error; rest the day before the exam.

**Every day, already happening:** English podcasts and news on the commute, documentation and notes in English, commits in English.
