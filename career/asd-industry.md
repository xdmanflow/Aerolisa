# The aerospace, space & defence (ASD) landscape

The long-term goal: data & AI specialist in the biggest ASD companies, Europe or US. This file keeps the map in one place. Update it when I learn something concrete (a team, a platform, a hiring pattern).

## Constraints to know early

- **Defence work often needs a security clearance.** In France: eligibility for a national habilitation, decided by the authorities, is written into many Airbus Defence and Space, Thales, MBDA, Naval and Dassault defence postings.
- **US defence and space are mostly closed to non-US persons.** Export-control rules (ITAR, EAR) and clearances mean most roles at Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon, SpaceX and NASA (civil-servant jobs) require US citizenship or US-person status. Read each posting; don't plan a career on an exception.
- **Realistic routes to the US:** commercial aerospace (engines, commercial aircraft, services, digital), and internal mobility inside a European group with US sites (Airbus has US sites and US data roles).
- **Data may never leave its network** in defence and some space programmes: on-premise platforms, classified networks, no external AI APIs. Being comfortable with those constraints is a plus in interviews.

## What transfers everywhere
Python, SQL, Spark, data modeling, ML for time series and reliability, MLOps, clear communication, and domain vocabulary. Foundry specifically is used by Airbus and by many defence and government organisations, which makes it a portable skill across the sector.
