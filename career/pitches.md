# Pitches

Beats to hit, in order. I write the actual words myself (first version without AI), record, rewatch, rewrite. English and French versions of each.

## "Tell me about yourself" (60–90 s)
1. Who I am in one line: engineering student in computer science, AI & data, CESI Toulouse, programme in English.
2. What I've done that's relevant: ALTEN data & AI internship (one outcome); SAA predictive maintenance research.
3. What I build on my own: Aerolisa in one sentence, with the Foundry edition.
4. What I want: a data apprenticeship on Skywise, because… (one real reason).
5. Bridge to them: "which is why this posting caught my attention: …"

## "Why Airbus?" (60 s)
1. Something specific about Airbus, not generic ("aviation is my passion" alone is not enough).
2. The data angle: Skywise as the industry's data platform, used by airlines and inside Airbus.
3. One Airbus value I already live, with a 1-line example.
4. Long-term: build a career in aerospace data & AI, starting here.

## "Why Skywise / why data?" (60 s)
1. What I find interesting in turning fleet data into maintenance decisions.
2. Proof I've already gone that way: Aerolisa + Foundry edition.
3. What I want to learn from the team.

## Aerolisa, three lengths
**30 seconds:** the problem (which engines to inspect first and why), the data (public: NASA engine simulations + real flight cycles), the result (a ranked, explained inspection queue), the stack in five words (PySpark, ontology, ML, Foundry, LLM copilot).

**2 minutes:** + architecture in one sentence per layer; one number (model vs baseline); one hard problem I solved; what I'd do with real airline data.

**5 minutes (with screen share):** + live demo path: fleet overview → one risky engine → why (explanation) → schedule inspection (action) → same flow in Foundry Workshop → copilot answering with sources. Script: Aerolisa [docs/demo-script.md](https://github.com/xdmanflow/Aerolisa/blob/main/docs/demo-script.md).

## Closing (15 s)
Thank them, restate interest in one sentence, ask about next steps.
